Delete your FFMPA account

FFMPA Kids News is made by Founderfreedom Ventures LLP. A parent or guardian can delete the family account and all of its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Delete it in the app (straight away)

  1. Open FFMPA on your phone or at ffmpa.com/app and sign in with your email.
  2. Tap the menu button, then Grown-ups, and enter your 4-digit PIN.
  3. In Parent settings, find Your account and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Yes, delete everything to confirm.

You can also tap Download my data first if you want a copy.

Or ask us by email

Write to hello@ffmpa.com from the email address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We reply to confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Your child's first name or nickname, settings, reading progress, quiz scores and streaks
  • Young reporter reports your child wrote
  • Classes you run as a teacher, and your child's place in any class
  • Edition alert subscriptions for your devices

What we keep, and for how long

Nothing linked to your family is kept after deletion. Our hosting and email providers keep security logs, which can include an email address or IP address, for up to 90 days before they are removed automatically. Class totals a teacher has already seen are counts only and never named a child.
