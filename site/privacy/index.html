Privacy policy

In short: we collect the least we can. A grown-up's email, a child's first name or nickname (optional), a reading level, a country and preferences. No personalised or targeted ads, no selling of data, no tracking across other sites, no profiling of children, no photos of your child and no precise location.

1. Who we are

FFMPA (ffmpa.com) is operated by Founderfreedom Ventures LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in India, based in Bangalore, India ("we", "us"). We are the data fiduciary (India) and data controller (UK and EU) for personal data processed through FFMPA.

2. Who FFMPA is for

FFMPA is a news service for children aged 5 to 15, used under the account and supervision of a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over. Accounts can only be created by an adult. Children cannot create accounts or sign in on their own.

4. Parental consent

Before any child profile is created, the parent or guardian verifies their email address with a one-time code and confirms that they are the child's parent or guardian. This is how we obtain verifiable consent as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (United States) and UK data protection law. The DPDP Rules, 2025 set out how a parent's identity and adult status must be verified, with the child data provisions due to apply from May 2027; we will add any extra verification step they require before then.

5. What we never do

  • We never use children's data for advertising. Any sponsor card is chosen by our team, labelled Sponsored, and shown the same way to every child in a country. If we ever use an outside ad partner, it receives no personal data and must use child-directed settings.
  • We never track children's behaviour across other websites or apps, or build profiles about them.
  • We never sell or rent personal data.

6. Who we share data with

When a child shares a story, only a public link to that story is shared; no personal data goes with it. Otherwise we share data only with service providers who help us run FFMPA, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only for us:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Stores your account and your child's progress, in Mumbai, India.
  • Cloudflare: delivers the website and app and protects it from attacks. Sees technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in code email. Receives your email address only.
  • GitHub: stores the published daily editions. Holds no personal data.
  • Google Analytics: counts visits to the grown-up website pages only, and only if you choose Allow. Never used in the app children read.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

7. Where data is stored

Account and progress data is stored by Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India. The website is delivered through Cloudflare's global network, and sign-in emails are sent through Resend, so some technical data and your email address may be processed outside India. Where data is transferred across borders, we use the safeguards required by applicable law.

8. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while the account is active. When you choose Delete account in parent settings, your account, your child's progress, reports and any classes you run are deleted straight away. If you ask us by email instead, we delete within 30 days. Our providers keep security logs for up to 90 days.

9. Your rights

A parent or guardian can at any time access, correct or delete their own and their child's data, withdraw consent (which closes the child profile), and nominate another person to exercise these rights. You can download or delete your data yourself in parent settings, or write to hello@ffmpa.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the US Federal Trade Commission.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, one-time sign-in codes that expire after 10 minutes, database rules that let each family read only its own data, and strict access controls for our team. The grown-ups PIN is a simple lock against children changing settings; it is not a password, so please do not reuse one from elsewhere.

11. Grievance Officer

Ashish Khandelwal, Grievance Officer, Founderfreedom Ventures LLP, Bangalore, India. Email: hello@ffmpa.com (subject: Grievance). We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects how we use data, we will email the account holder before the change takes effect. Last updated: 13 November 2026.
